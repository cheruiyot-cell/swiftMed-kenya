Insurance & Payment

Wait Times & Efficiency

Zero minutes. That's our promise. You book a specific time slot, you arrive at that time, and the doctor sees you immediately. The live ticker at the top shows current wait times – you'll see it's always 0.

We use a strict booking system that allocates exact time slots for each patient. Our doctors are never double-booked, and we prepare your consultation in advance. If we're ever running late, we'll let you know immediately and offer an alternative.

We highly recommend booking to guarantee a slot. However, if you walk in, we'll check the live waitlist. If there's a free slot, we'll see you right away; otherwise we'll book you for the next available time – it's usually within 30 minutes.

Teleconsult

After booking, you'll receive a video call link via WhatsApp/SMS. At your scheduled time, click the link and you'll be connected to your doctor. You'll get a prescription and any follow-up notes digitally.

Yes. If the doctor orders tests, you can visit our lab at your convenience – no need to book. You'll receive results via WhatsApp within a few hours.
