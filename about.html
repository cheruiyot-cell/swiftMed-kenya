Why swiftMed Exists

Every Nairobian knows the pain: you wake up feeling unwell, take a matatu to the clinic, then wait for hours in a crowded waiting room – only to be rushed through a five-minute consult. Your time is valuable, but the system doesn't respect it.

We started swiftMed in 2024 with a simple idea: healthcare should fit around your life, not the other way around. So we built a clinic that runs on exact time slots, instant insurance verification, and a digital-first experience that puts you in control.

Today, we're Nairobi's fastest-growing clinic, with a 4.9-star rating and a waiting time of zero minutes. Our patients walk in, see the doctor, and walk out – often within 20 minutes.

Our Mission

To make healthcare fast, transparent, and accessible for every Kenyan – by eliminating queues, simplifying insurance, and providing world-class care on demand.

Our Vision

A Kenya where no one has to miss a day of work just to see a doctor – where healthcare is efficient and affordable.

What We Stand For

These values guide every decision we make.

Respect for Your Time

Your time is non-negotiable. We schedule precisely, we never overbook, and we apologize if we ever run late.

Transparent Care

You'll always know the cost before treatment, your insurance cover is confirmed upfront, and you get a clear diagnosis.

Human Connection

We treat people, not numbers. Our doctors listen first, and they speak in language you understand – no jargon.

Privacy & Trust

Your medical records are encrypted and only shared with your care team. We comply with the Data Protection Act.
